## What is it?

Inflation measures how much the prices of the things families buy went up in
one year. It includes food, rent, transport, medicine and much more.

## Why does it matter?

When prices rise faster than wages, the same money buys less at the end of the
month. With 5% inflation, R$ 100 of shopping costs about R$ 105.

## How to read the chart?

The purple line is Brazil. The green band is the target the government set for
each year. If the line is inside the green band, prices rose at the planned
pace. The target started in 1999. Before that, there was no target to compare
with.

## Where does it come from?

From IBGE, Brazil's statistics office, every month. The technical name is
IPCA. The target is set by the National Monetary Council and published by the
Central Bank.
