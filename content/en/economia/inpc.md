## Why does it matter?

The INPC is used to adjust the minimum wage and many pensions. So it helps
explain how much the minimum wage goes up each year. Families with less money
also spend a bigger share on food, rent and transport. When those prices rise
faster, the INPC goes above the IPCA.

## What is it?

The INPC measures how much the prices of the things lower-income families buy
went up. It covers families earning 1 to 5 minimum wages. The official
inflation rate, the IPCA, covers families earning 1 to 40 minimum wages.

## How to read the chart?

The purple line is the INPC. The grey line is the IPCA. When the purple line
is above the grey one, prices rose more for lower-income families. There is no
warning colour: the government sets no target for the INPC.

## Where does it come from?

From IBGE, Brazil's statistics office, every month. The technical name is
INPC, the National Consumer Price Index.
